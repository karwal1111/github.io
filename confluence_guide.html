01 / CORE

One trade. Five witnesses.

No single indicator is the strategy. Each of the five lenses answers a different question. You take the trade only when at least four say yes — the same four, at the same price, at the same time.
Lens 01

Momentum

RSI, MACD, ADX across HTF/LTF. Is the trend alive? Is the pullback ending?

Lens 02

Divergence

Price vs oscillator disagreement. Regular = exhaustion. Hidden = continuation.

Lens 03

IFVG

A gap that flipped. Support that became resistance. Proof of order-flow shift.

Lens 04

Elliott Wave

Are we in Wave 2/4 (buy dip) or Wave 5 done (fade trend)?

Lens 05

Fibonacci

Statistical pullback zones (38.2 / 50 / 61.8 / 78.6). Where dips become opportunity.

The Gate

4 of 5 agree

Fewer than 4 lenses aligned = no trade. The edge lives in the waiting, not the clicking.

02 / LENS 03

Fair Value Gap → Inverse Fair Value Gap

A three-candle pattern leaves a price gap — an imbalance. When price returns and closes fully through the gap, its polarity flips. Support becomes resistance. That flip, when re-tested, is your entry zone.

// TRAP — THE MICRO-FVG Any gap smaller than 0.15% of price on NIFTY/BANKNIFTY is noise. On a 24,500 NIFTY that's ~37 points. Smaller than that, and random candle wicks will "invert" it hourly. You will take 12 fake trades a week. Filter aggressively — big gaps or no gap.
// TRAP — THE WICK THROUGH A wick piercing the FVG is NOT an inversion. You need a full 1H candle close on the opposite side. If you trade wick pokes, liquidity hunters will eat you every single time.

04 / LENS 04

Elliott Wave — Reversal or Correction?

The most expensive question in trading: is this pullback a Wave 2/4 (buy) or a post-Wave-5 reversal (sell)? Same chart shape, opposite trade. Use the rules — Wave 2 doesn't break Wave 1 start; Wave 3 is never shortest; Wave 4 doesn't overlap Wave 1.

// TRAP — THE B-WAVE After a five-wave impulse, a three-wave A-B-C correction follows. Wave B often rallies almost as high as the original top. Beginners buy Wave B thinking "breakout continuation." Then Wave C crushes them. Rule: after a completed five with regular divergence, the next rally is the short, not the long.

05 / LENS 02

Divergence — the oscillator's confession

When price disagrees with RSI or MACD, one of them is lying. Regular divergence = trend is tiring (reversal). Hidden divergence = pullback will fail (continuation). Only trust it on 1H or higher — below that it's noise.

09 / SESSION

Indian market — session by session

The NSE day is not uniform. Different windows have different edges. The playbook respects that.
08:30 – 09:15
Pre-market prepMark HTF bias, open FVGs, Fib levels, pending Elliott counts. Pick 1–2 setups worth watching today.
09:15 – 09:30
Opening imbalance — NO NEW ENTRIESGaps + overnight global cues distort everything. Let the noise clear.
09:30 – 11:30
Primary trend session — BEST WINDOWTechnique A and C entries shine here. Real volume, real signals.
11:30 – 12:30
First lullManage open trades. Move SL to breakeven if T1 hit. Don't force new entries on dead candles.
12:30 – 14:00
Lunch chop — trap zoneFalse breakouts multiply. Avoid breakouts. Pullback entries only if confluence is A-grade, sized down 25%.
14:00 – 15:00
Institutional session — SECOND-BEST WINDOWReal positioning finalises. Trend resumes or a counter-IFVG forms — reassess bias.
15:00 – 15:20
Pre-closeNo new weekly options. Overnight holds only if A-grade.
15:20 – 15:30
Close — journal timeRecord closes. Update journal. No panic trading into the bell.
