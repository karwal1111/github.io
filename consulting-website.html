Our Methodology

Hiring, Engineered Like Software

Every engagement follows our 6-phase SDLC-inspired talent delivery framework — structured, measurable, and repeatable.

1
Discovery
Deep-dive requirement gathering with stakeholders. Define roles, team gaps, culture, and success metrics.
2
Planning
Build a talent roadmap. Define timelines, sourcing strategy, evaluation criteria, and risk buffers.
3
Sourcing
Active headhunting, network mining, and job market campaigns to fill a qualified pipeline.
4
Screening
Multi-layer evaluation: skills assessment, behavioral interviews, culture alignment, and reference checks.
5
Deployment
Offer facilitation, negotiation support, and seamless handoff to your team with full documentation.
6
Review
30/60/90-day post-placement tracking and retrospectives to continuously improve our process.
